- (decision) [ADOPTED] Add optional direct Q capability discovery on 0x7F. With zero AUX/DCB length and no queued NIO transaction, it returns wire revision 1, little-endian device maximum payload, and feature flags; bit 0 advertises advisory PROC readiness support. Q neither starts nor consumes W/P/R state. The public Atari library uses the fixed 512-byte baseline unless an application explicitly opts in, then limits itself to the lesser of its compiled capacity and Q's maximum. This is isolated from D1-D8 boot.
- (decision) ADOPTED: Q is legal only at idle with zero AUX/DCB length; all v1 devices support the 512-byte baseline, while lower-capacity profiles require a newer wire revision and matching fixtures. This keeps discovery optional.
